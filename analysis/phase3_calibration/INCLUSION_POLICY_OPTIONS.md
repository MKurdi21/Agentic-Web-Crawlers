# Inclusion Policy Options

Status: recommendation pending owner approval.

The knowledge base and any synthesis denominator are separate decisions. Registering a report preserves it; it does not make the report an independent experimental contribution.

## Proposed roles

| Role | Knowledge-base use | Default synthesis eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| `CORE` | Directly addresses agentic web systems, risks, or defenses | Eligible after required verification |
| `SUPPORTING` | Supplies methods, mechanisms, or adjacent evidence | Eligible only for a named supporting question |
| `CONTEXTUAL` | Provides background needed to interpret core evidence | Excluded from primary contribution denominator |
| `SURVEY_SYNTHESIS` | Supports taxonomy and prior-work orientation | Excluded from primary experimental denominator |
| `TRADITIONAL_CRAWLING_BACKGROUND` | Establishes non-agent crawling concepts | Eligible only for historical/background questions |
| `MANUAL_REVIEW` | Relevance or contribution unit remains uncertain | Ineligible pending adjudication |
| `EXCLUDED` | Outside the approved question | Retained with exclusion reason; ineligible |

## Recommendation

Adopt role-based inclusion plus per-synthesis eligibility records. Each eligibility decision must name the synthesis question, report or research-object unit, verified evidence fingerprint, inclusion rationale, and policy version. Do not infer role from folders alone. Surveys may inform taxonomy without counting as independent primary contributions. Version-linked reports remain separate evidence sources until research-object adjudication.

Owner approval is required for final scope, role definitions, and the authority permitted to change them.
