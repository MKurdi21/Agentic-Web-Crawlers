# Gap Scoring Policy Options

Status: recommendation pending owner approval.

- `NO_NUMERIC_SCORE`: evidence-backed narrative gap candidates only.
- `ORDINAL_PRIORITY`: transparent low/medium/high triage with explicit criteria.
- `TRANSPARENT_WEIGHTED_HEURISTIC`: published factors and weights, sensitivity analysis, and human review.
- `EMPIRICALLY_CALIBRATED_MODEL`: requires a sufficiently large labeled set, held-out evaluation, governance, and drift monitoring.

## Recommendation

Keep numeric gap scoring disabled during initial verified synthesis. Twelve development categories cannot establish authoritative weights. Gap candidates must trace to a frozen synthesis, eligible verified evidence, and explicit uncertainty. No global novelty claim is allowed without a separately authorized external-validation protocol.
