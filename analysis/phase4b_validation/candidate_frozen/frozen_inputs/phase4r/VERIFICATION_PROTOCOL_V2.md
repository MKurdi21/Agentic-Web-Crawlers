# Source verification protocol, version 2

Verify every synthesis-critical quantitative, comparative, derived, negative, identity, model/dataset/baseline, security-assumption, and interpretation-limitation proposition. Lower-risk sampling remains a separate frozen-policy decision; sampling never substitutes for critical review.

For each proposition ask: Does the **specified support locator** entail it? Is the number correct and reported or derived? If derived, can exact located operands be recomputed? Is comparison scope explicit and document-wide coverage sufficient for “overall” or “best”? Are conditions, units, and qualifiers preserved? Does an appendix or later section narrow or contradict it? Does every material proposition in a compound claim have support? Are negative, zero, missing, untested, and unreported states distinguished?

Record primary, separate-context model verifier, and source adjudication independently. A disagreement is never silently converted to support. `SEPARATE_CONTEXT_MODEL_VERIFICATION` is not `INDEPENDENT_HUMAN_SOURCE_REVIEW`; neither it nor a test fixture is `TRUSTED_HUMAN_APPROVAL`. No Phase 4R process may set live `SOURCE_VERIFIED` or `PROMOTED`. Without qualified human ground truth, `ground_truth_critical_false_accepts` remains `UNKNOWN`.

The candidate's deterministic checks reject missing scope, arithmetic mismatches, incomplete atomic coverage, and context-only support records. They do not prove the underlying source transcription true. A source reviewer must inspect relevant PDF text and rendered pages, including tables, figures, and appendices.
