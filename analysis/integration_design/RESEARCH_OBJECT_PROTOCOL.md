# Research Object Protocol

Preserve separate reports until contribution relationships are adjudicated. Current2 candidate pairs are unresolved; hash-equal duplicate copies are physical observations,not new scientific contributions. Relationship proposals require members,relationship type,evidence references and rationale. CONFIRMED membership requires trusted researcher adjudication; filename/folder/title similarity cannot confirm it.

Membership history must retain decisions and supersession; do not mutate historical summaries to make them fit a new grouping. Synthesis inputs carry current object identities and exact version fingerprints. Contribution-aware denominators require confirmed coverage of the included report set. Unresolved groups block definitive object-level synthesis by default. Report-level descriptive inventories remain possible with explicit unresolved limitations,without calling them verified synthesis.

The shadow importer creates relationships only and no confirmed objects. Runtime resolution contracts exist and require verification predecessors/human gate. Broad contribution policy and adjudicator identity remain open; this phase does not decide actual report-version deltas.
