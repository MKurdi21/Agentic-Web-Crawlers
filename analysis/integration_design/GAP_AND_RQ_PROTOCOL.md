# Gap And Rq Protocol

Gaps may be registered without a numeric score. The reference weighted score is not adopted as an authoritative project policy: historical guidance uses qualitative judgment and the audit leaves scoring unresolved. Each gap traces to current synthesis records,an explicit statement,limitations,and NOT_EXTERNALLY_VALIDATED status.

Future external validation must record search source/query/date,inclusion decisions,evidence found,conflicting claims,coverage limitations and exact candidate version. It requires separate authorization. No external novelty search or paper metadata update is performed now. Architecture web research is separate and recorded in WEB_ARCHITECTURE_SOURCES.md.

Each research question traces through existing gap IDs to synthesis and reviewed source evidence; it records a testable outcome and limitations. Gap/RQ text must not claim confirmed novelty merely because a schema passes. Final ranking,search scope,verification threshold and research agenda remain human decisions.
