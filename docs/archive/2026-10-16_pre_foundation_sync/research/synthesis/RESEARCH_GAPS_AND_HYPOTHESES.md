# Research gaps, contributions and falsifiable hypotheses

## 1. Scope and prioritization

These proposals arise from the 112-record corpus and the [critical review](COMPREHENSIVE_LITERATURE_REVIEW.md). “Gap” means a question not adequately resolved by the reviewed evidence, not proof of global absence. Existing implementations and protocols must be checked in primary artifacts before final novelty claims. No experiment is implemented here.

The recommended question is: **How do web observation, delegated tool authority and persistent state change exposure to indirect prompt injection, instruction-following deviations and verified harmful outcomes relative to a matched standalone language-model configuration?** This permits improved, unchanged or worsened susceptibility, and separates it from exposure and consequences.

Priorities balance scientific significance, identifiable estimands, feasible sandbox evidence and distinction from existing work. They are editorial judgments, not a quantitative novelty score.

| Priority | Candidate contribution | Significance / feasibility | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
|1| Paired exposure–decision–execution decomposition | High explanatory value; feasible with frozen content and a sandbox; limited initial task scope. | A superficially matched comparison may still change prompts, information or reachable objectives. |
|2| Stage-aware endpoint and evaluator-validity audit | High methodological value; feasible on a carefully selected task set and accessible traces. | Access/licensing and annotation cost; raw artifacts may be absent. |
|3| Memory acquisition versus persistence experiment | High importance; moderate feasibility in a controlled memory service. | Stores/retrieval and attack privileges are easily bundled. |
|4| Enforcement/utility frontier under adaptive attacks | High practical importance; moderate feasibility for selected controls. | Comparing implementations with unmatched assumptions or attacker budgets. |
|5| Matched visual/HTML/caption intervention | Useful modality-specific explanation; moderate effort. | Semantic equivalence and perturbation realism are hard to establish. |
|6| Resource safeguards and utility-preserving limits | Concrete operational value; feasible with instrumented sandbox tools. | Caps can trivially stop both useful work and attacks. |

A successful contribution need not show that agents are more susceptible. A precise null effect, an exposure-mediated effect or a consequence-only effect can correct a misleading research premise.

## 2. Contribution1: paired causal decomposition

**Related work.** Toolformer enables/disables calls within one trained model for capability; Overthinking Loops changes a registry within agents; Context Manipulation corrupts stored state; CaMeL/ceLLMate intervene on execution boundaries. WebGPT, BrowseComp and other capability comparisons change training, model or endpoint. These establish useful factors without an otherwise matched adversarial decomposition. [Toolformer](../records/toolformer_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims toolformer_claim_01 through toolformer_claim_16; physical PDF pp.1–17); [Overthinking Loops](../records/overthinking_loops_in_agents_a_structural_risk_via_mcp_tools_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.1–12); [Context Manipulation](../records/context_manipulation_attacks_web_agents_are_susceptible_to_corrupted_memory_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.4–8); [CaMeL](../records/defeating_prompt_injections_by_design_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C12; physical PDF pp.1–40); [ceLLMate](../records/cellmate_sandboxing_browser_ai_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims cellmate_c01 through cellmate_c15; physical PDF pp.2–13); [WebGPT](../records/webgpt_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims webgpt_c01 through webgpt_c15; physical PDF pp.1–28); [BrowseComp](../records/browsecomp_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims browsecomp_c01 through browsecomp_c16; physical PDF pp.1–11).

**Unanswered question.** At fixed model, user goal, untrusted content, attack, exposure, budget and sampling policy, which interface/context changes alter decision deviations, and which executor/permission changes alter effects? A separate question is how autonomous routing changes encounter probability. These cannot be answered by comparing naturally diverging trajectories and calling everything “agentization.”

**Why it matters/evidence.** WASP's intermediate/final divergence and InjecAgent's validity denominators demonstrate how different stage choices alter conclusions. Fixed source exposure often removes the very routing process invoked as the reason agents are risky. Conversely, allowing routing to vary makes a susceptibility contrast post-treatment selected. [WASP](../records/wasp_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims wasp_c01 through wasp_c16; physical PDF pp.3–22); [InjecAgent](../records/injecagent_benchmarking_indirect_prompt_injections_in_tool_integrated_large_language_model_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.1–34); [Retrieval Barrier](../records/retrieval_barrier_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C18; physical PDF pp.1–20).

**Investigation.** Use a paired replay experiment with a common decision contract, then a distinct closed-loop sandbox experiment. Compare text-only decision, web-observation decision and executor-enabled variants; vary memory/horizon orthogonally in an extension. Measure deviation, proposed forbidden action, accepted execution and verified side effect separately. Include clean pairs and benign-but-instructional content. See the [study design](CONTROLLED_COMPARISON_STUDY_DESIGN.md).

**Distinction.** The contribution is an estimand and endpoint decomposition with controlled exposure, not another high-ASR attack set. It explicitly reports what ordinary standalone behavior cannot measure, rather than declaring impossible actions safely resisted.

**External verification required.** Search primary work on agent/non-agent safety comparisons, tool-access ablations, exposure-conditioned attacks and matched interface experiments; inspect original protocols behind survey tool-access claims. Check whether the exact contrasts, artifact controls and common endpoint already exist. Neither a new name nor a larger benchmark establishes novelty.

## 3. Contribution2: endpoint reliability and evaluator audit

**Related work.** AgentDojo uses mutable state, WASP intermediate/final checks, SecureWebArena reasoning/behavior/delivery and VPI-Bench attempts/completion. AgentRewardBench, GUIDE and An Illusion of Progress evaluate judges rather than adversarial endpoint validity. SafeArena illustrates divergence between refusal, attempts and functional harmful completion. [AgentDojo](../records/agentdojo_a_dynamic_environment_to_evaluate_prompt_injection_attacks_and_defenses_for_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.1–26); [WASP](../records/wasp_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims wasp_c01 through wasp_c16; physical PDF pp.3–22); [SecureWebArena](../records/securewebarena_a_holistic_security_evaluation_benchmark_for_lvlm_based_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01_dual_source_threat_model through C12_research_relevance; physical PDF pp.3–13); [VPI-Bench](../records/vpi_bench_visual_prompt_injection_attacks_for_computer_use_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims vpi_claim_01 through vpi_claim_17; physical PDF pp.2–21); [AgentRewardBench](../records/agentrewardbench_evaluating_automatic_evaluations_of_web_agent_trajectories_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.3–23); [GUIDE](../records/guide_interpretable_gui_agent_evaluation_via_hierarchical_diagnosis_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims guide_claim_01 through guide_claim_14; physical PDF pp.1–8); [An Illusion of Progress](../records/an_illusion_of_progress_assessing_the_current_state_of_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01_benchmark through claim_16_no_comparative_security_test; physical PDF pp.3–30); [SafeArena](../records/safearena_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims SA_C01 through SA_C15; physical PDF pp.1–37).

**Unanswered question.** How often would conclusions about relative vulnerability or defense utility change if response/trajectory judging were replaced by independently checkable side effects and if all assigned cases remained in the denominator? Which errors depend on model, task competence, attack family or judge access?

**Why it matters/evidence.** InjecAgent simulates extraction continuation; Hidden in Memory's uptake includes claims and attempts; some defenses obtain low ASR by aborting. These are useful measures but support different conclusions. Judge precision on predicted positives is not whole-population false-positive rate. [InjecAgent](../records/injecagent_benchmarking_indirect_prompt_injections_in_tool_integrated_large_language_model_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.1–34); [Hidden in Memory](../records/hidden_in_memory_sleeper_memory_poisoning_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.3–80); [DoomArena](../records/doomarena_a_framework_for_testing_ai_agents_against_evolving_security_threats_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.1–25).

**Investigation.** Predefine an endpoint ladder, independently annotate a stratified sample including failures/refusals, compare judge outputs with sandbox logs, report confusion matrices and uncertainty by stage, and perform sensitivity analyses for disputed labels. Record crashes and invalid outputs as their own outcomes rather than silently removing them. Do not retrospectively relabel a paper's metric as if its authors measured execution.

**Distinction.** Unlike a generic judge leaderboard, the audit studies whether measurement changes a causal security conclusion. Its primary output is a quantified disagreement/estimand analysis, not a single accuracy score.

**External verification required.** Check available execution-grounded agent-safety benchmark audits, judge-calibration work and trace datasets; verify licenses, original evaluator implementations and whether source discrepancies reflect version changes. Reproducing a known evaluator flaw is not global methodological novelty.

## 4. Contribution3: memory acquisition, retrieval and persistence

**Related work.** Context Manipulation assumes direct writes; Hidden in Memory studies environmental acquisition and downstream uptake; WASP carries instructions in URLs; AgentLAB composes multi-turn attacks. SkillTrojan persists through installed packages while fragment state is run-local. LASM distinguishes temporal classes, but its literature assignments do not estimate persistence probability. [Context Manipulation](../records/context_manipulation_attacks_web_agents_are_susceptible_to_corrupted_memory_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.4–8); [Hidden in Memory](../records/hidden_in_memory_sleeper_memory_poisoning_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.3–80); [WASP](../records/wasp_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims wasp_c01 through wasp_c16; physical PDF pp.3–22); [AgentLAB](../records/agentlab_benchmarking_llm_agents_against_long_horizon_attacks_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01_benchmark_scope through claim_14_shopping_drift; physical PDF pp.1–19); [SkillTrojan](../records/skilltrojan_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–14); [LASM](../records/a_systematic_survey_of_security_threats_and_defenses_in_llm_based_ai_agents_a_layered_attack_surface_framework_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01_architecture through claim_14_multiplicity; physical PDF pp.1–57).

**Unanswered question.** Given a fixed model and downstream task, what proportion of attacks acquire unauthorized state, survive a reset, become retrieved, change a decision and cause an executed effect? How much does control at write time differ from control at read time? Does verified provenance at one stage remain attached after summarization and transfer?

**Why it matters/evidence.** Conditional uptake among already poisoned/retrieved memories omits acquisition and selection. Direct state edits bypass the first barrier. Package persistence must not be presented as a cross-session memory-write result.

**Investigation.** Factor acquisition route, write permission, retrieval policy and delay; use fresh session resets and benign memory controls. Compare matched stored content with/without authentic provenance, and measure each stage plus all-task joint outcomes. Randomize memory assignments before downstream tasks. Keep cross-session duration and amount of interaction as separate variables. Full-system outcomes should not be estimated merely by multiplying conditional rates from incompatible cohorts.

**Distinction.** This is an end-to-end state-transition experiment with fixed contents and explicit privileges, not merely another attack against a memory interface. Proposed provenance controls must be tested for utility and false rejection.

**External verification required.** Inspect recent persistent-memory attacks, memory provenance systems, forgetting/correction defenses and provider-native implementations. Check whether corresponding write/read factorial experiments already exist. Production reconstruction can otherwise misstate the true mechanism.

## 5. Contribution4: adaptive enforcement and utility frontiers

**Related work.** WARD/WebAgentGuard screen observations; Task Shield/PlanGuard judge semantics; MELON re-executes masked context; IsolateGPT, ceLLMate, CaMeL, ACE, UCM and Prismata enforce different boundaries. The corpus's attack assumptions, label policies and utility endpoints differ. [WARD](../records/ward_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims ward_c01 through ward_c15; physical PDF pp.2–22); [WebAgentGuard](../records/webagentguard_a_reasoning_driven_guard_model_for_detecting_prompt_injection_attacks_in_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.2–18); [Task Shield](../records/the_task_shield_enforcing_task_alignment_to_defend_against_indirect_prompt_injection_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_12; physical PDF pp.1–15); [PlanGuard](../records/planguard_defending_agents_against_indirect_prompt_injection_via_planning_based_consistency_verification_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims PG_C01 through PG_C17; physical PDF pp.1–6); [MELON](../records/melon_provable_defense_against_indirect_prompt_injection_attacks_in_ai_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.2–18); [IsolateGPT](../records/isolategpt_an_execution_isolation_architecture_for_llm_based_agentic_systems_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01_shared_execution_risk through claim_14_implementation_controls; physical PDF pp.1–19); [ceLLMate](../records/cellmate_sandboxing_browser_ai_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims cellmate_c01 through cellmate_c15; physical PDF pp.2–13); [CaMeL](../records/defeating_prompt_injections_by_design_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C12; physical PDF pp.1–40); [ACE](../records/ace_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims ace_c01 through ace_c18; physical PDF pp.1–18); [UCM](../records/untrusted_content_masking_for_web_agents_with_security_guarantees_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C12; physical PDF pp.1–22); [Prismata](../records/prismata_confining_cross_site_prompt_injection_in_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C1 through C13; physical PDF pp.2–15).

**Unanswered question.** At comparable clean utility and task coverage, which boundary prevents specified sandbox effects under a budget-matched adaptive adversary? Which allowed operations still permit misuse? How does policy/label error affect both legitimate tasks and protection?

**Why it matters/evidence.** IsolateGPT requires correct warning decisions; UCM requires correct labels; ceLLMate depends on trusted semantic policies; conditional guarantees do not prove implementation completeness. Low ASR/zero utility in aborting defenses prevents interpreting lower ASR as uniformly better protection.

**Investigation.** Evaluate a small set of reproducible mechanisms in one sandbox with shared targets. Separate fixed-payload transfer from independently optimized per-defense attacks; the latter changes the attack distribution and answers a different question. Report full security/utility/latency curves and an abstain-all control. Inject controlled policy errors only as an explicit robustness experiment, not as a reason to dismiss correct-policy guarantees.

**Distinction.** The contribution is a matched enforcement-boundary analysis with side-effect endpoints and meaningful utility controls. It is not a new guard architecture or a claim that all mechanisms are interchangeable.

**External verification required.** Check head-to-head containment studies, original implementations/guarantees, adaptive benchmark protocols and user-permission research. A comparison cannot claim fairness if one defense receives additional trusted labels or a weaker attacker without disclosure.

## 6. Contribution5: modality and preprocessing mediation

**Related work.** AdvAgent/EIA exploit HTML grounding; Pop-ups and DUDE emphasize visual clicks; Dissecting separates captioner/actor; WebInject assumes white-box pixel optimization; BrowseSafe and SnapGuard evaluate different detector channels. [AdvAgent](../records/advagent_controllable_blackbox_red_teaming_on_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–13); [EIA](../records/eia_environmental_injection_attack_on_generalist_web_agents_for_privacy_leakage_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims eia_claim_01 through eia_claim_14; physical PDF pp.4–32); [Pop-ups](../records/attacking_vision_language_computer_agents_via_pop_ups_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_15; physical PDF pp.2–15); [DUDE](../records/dont_click_that_teaching_web_agents_to_resist_deceptive_interfaces_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_14; physical PDF pp.1–19); [Dissecting Adversarial Robustness](../records/dissecting_adversarial_robustness_of_multimodal_lm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–22); [WebInject](../records/webinject_prompt_injection_attack_to_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–21); [BrowseSafe](../records/browsesafe_understanding_and_preventing_prompt_injection_within_ai_browser_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims bs_claim_01 through bs_claim_16; physical PDF pp.2–25); [SnapGuard](../records/snapguard_lightweight_prompt_injection_detection_for_screenshot_based_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims snapguard_c01 through snapguard_c13; physical PDF pp.1–9).

**Unanswered question.** Holding attacker intent, task and visible semantic content fixed, which preprocessing/transcription boundary changes the route from perception to unauthorized decision? Is apparent robustness due to failing to perceive relevant content or to respecting authority after perception?

**Investigation.** Pair HTML, screenshot, accessibility and caption representations of the same controlled page; independently annotate semantic equivalence and visibility. Separate source modification from full-screenshot control. Compare actor outcomes after identical canonical captions to estimate downstream effects, alongside a full-pipeline total effect. Report when matching cannot be achieved instead of treating different representations as equivalent.

**Significance/distinction.** This separates modality mediation from task/model or attacker-budget changes. It tests an explanation for heterogeneous results rather than another strongest-cell ASR. Equivalence is costly and task-limited, so this is a second-stage contribution rather than the first thesis experiment.

**External verification required.** Check cross-modal injection equivalence, accessibility security and captioner/actor ablations in original studies, including work outside web agents.

## 7. Contribution6: resource harm and usable bounds

**Related work.** AgentDoS studies resource sinks; IAL-SCAN code loops; Overthinking Loops registries; Beyond Max Tokens tool chains; RecurGuard streaming detection. Classical crawler/serverless results supply analogies, not LLM-specific estimates. [AgentDoS](../records/autonomy_comes_with_costs_detecting_denial_of_service_vulnerabilities_caused_by_resource_abusing_in_llm_based_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C13; physical PDF pp.4–15); [IAL-SCAN](../records/when_agents_do_not_stop_uncovering_infinite_agentic_loops_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims IAL_C01 through IAL_C14; physical PDF pp.2–10); [Overthinking Loops](../records/overthinking_loops_in_agents_a_structural_risk_via_mcp_tools_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.1–12); [Beyond Max Tokens](../records/beyond_max_tokens_stealthy_resource_amplification_via_tool_calling_chains_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.3–14); [RecurGuard](../records/recurguard_runtime_monitoring_for_reasoning_token_consumption_attacks_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.2–14); [IRLbot](../records/irlbot_scaling_to_6_billion_pagesand_beyond_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims irlbot_c01 through irlbot_c14; physical PDF pp.3–32); [Denial of Wallet](../records/denial_of_wallet_defining_a_looming_threat_to_serverless_computing_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–10).

**Unanswered question.** Which call/token/time/state quotas reduce harmful resource use without truncating equivalent benign work? How much amplification remains at a matched task-success target, and does a detector act before the expensive effect?

**Investigation.** Use clean/attacked matched tasks and identical tools; vary quotas explicitly; measure input/output tokens, tool work, wall time and completed user outcomes separately. Distinguish billed service units from hardware energy and projected money. Include timeouts and unsuccessful tasks in intention-to-treat accounting; success-only ratios are secondary. Denominators must remain stable across cap changes.

**Distinction.** A usable security/utility frontier and intervention timing would extend static loop alerts and unconstrained maxima. It would not establish a universal monetary risk or model-architecture effect.

**External verification required.** Check recent agent cost attacks, cancellation semantics, resource-policy evaluations and provider accounting before claiming originality or economic impact.

## 8. Operational hypotheses and competing explanations

| Hypothesis | Planned test | Falsifier or competing explanation |
|---|---|---|
|H1: Interface context changes decision susceptibility under common exposure. | Two-sided clean-adjusted paired interface contrast in deviation, with the same checkpoint/content/sampling and common decision schema; see the design primary interaction. | Null/equivalent result; effect disappears after representation/token-position controls. Does not identify changed intrinsic weights. |
|H2: Closed-loop routing changes exposure and total risk. | Randomized route/interface policy; report exposure and joint outcomes over all assigned tasks. | No encounter/risk difference; route changes utility rather than manipulation. Conditioning on encountered cases alone is not causal. |
|H3: External enforcement reduces effects independently of decision robustness. | Replay the same proposed-call sequence into policy-on/off sandbox executors. | No reduction or effect requires denying all useful actions; common calls avoid post-treatment decision selection. |
|H4: Memory acquisition and later retrieval are separate bottlenecks. | Factor write route/permission and retrieval with fixed content and fresh sessions. | No persistent effect, or change explained by retrieval rather than storage integrity. |
|H5: Fixed-payload and adaptive defense rankings can differ. | Separate attack-transfer and budget-matched per-defense optimization experiments. | Stable ordering with uncertainty; adaptive gains explained only by extra queries/selection. |
|H6: Low ASR can coexist with poor utility or high resource burden. | Joint all-task distribution of security, utility, false rejection, tokens and latency. | A matched control dominates with equal utility and lower burden; endpoint changes explain an apparent tradeoff. |

H1 and the proposed primary study are two-sided. Directional resource or policy expectations are hypotheses, not evidence that the original comparative premise must hold. Define a smallest relevant effect and equivalence region before confirmatory data; a nonsignificant result alone is not equivalence.

## 9. Evidence strength and missing controls

**Well supported within scope:** third-party content can redirect configured agents; attacker access and task alignment matter; stages from encounter to effect diverge; external enforcement can constrain specified effects; utility and evaluator choices materially qualify defense claims.

**Weaker or conflicting:** natural population encounter rates, universal relative vulnerability, general long-horizon monotonicity, unbounded adaptive robustness, many combined-defense claims, and extrapolated production harms. Survey coverage/severity ratings are not substitutes for causal experiments. Internal numerical conflicts further constrain precise effect claims.

**Controls most often needed:** same model snapshot and prompt contract; clean paired tasks; fixed versus natural exposure separation; equal attack optimization/transfer budgets; executor/permission ablations; independent side-effect checks; cluster-aware repeats; complete denominators including crashes and invalid outputs; utility/cost measures; independent development/test task families.

## 10. Recommended academic sequence

First verify the central gap externally through original papers and accessible artifacts, distinguishing matched security from tool-use capability. Then specify the primary estimand, threat model, common decision interface and sandbox endpoint definitions. A small feasibility/annotation pilot should estimate discordance, event prevalence, costs and task-family dependence; it should not optimize the confirmatory effect. Finalize sample size and analysis before a separate evaluation set. Extend to memory, modality or adaptive defense only after the core identification is credible.

The strongest defensible thesis claim would be a reproducible decomposition showing **which component changes which security quantity under stated conditions**, including credible nulls. A new universal “agents are more vulnerable” assertion, or a new acronym for an existing benchmark, would be weaker. The present deliverables support planning; implementation, ethics review where applicable and a publication manuscript are subsequent separate tasks.
