## Research question, comparisons and status

**Question:** Does task-relevant framing of recurring website prerequisites increase bounded, independently verified non-progress and sustained retention relative to neutral framing on otherwise matched, solvable browser workflows? Can progress-aware escape/recovery reduce this burden without sacrificing legitimate task utility?

C = clean benign task; NF = neutral + finite; TF = task-relevant + finite; NR = neutral + recurring; TR = task-relevant + recurring. These are stable names for existing contrasts. H1–H3, pilot arithmetic, budgets and the retained secondary comparison methodology were not changed. Matched goal facts, useful information, affordances, observation volume, delay, legitimate route and usable exit remain required; include truthful finite and genuinely iterative benign controls. Goal/escape reachability and oracle validity are proposed gates, not executed experiment results.

Separate served content, delivered observation, initial entry, continued decoy-directed actions, semantic recurrence, independently verified legitimate non-progress, safe stopping, escape, restoration/recovery, committed goal completion and resources/operational failure. Maintain all-assigned and conditional-on-entry denominators. Attacker-controlled UI/self-report is not trusted scoring; proposed and executed actions differ; missing traces/censored episodes and benign inability are not resistance. Finite caps do not prove literal infinity.

Agent traps, decoys, recurrent/recursive prerequisites, task-compatible pretexts, termination manipulation, semantic evaluation, behavioral controls and rollback already exist. Possible novelty is the particular controlled comparison and validated measurement, conditional on close competitor-method/artifact review. Novelty, feasibility, manipulation effects and utility-preserving recovery remain UNPROVEN. Neither Protocol V1.1 amendment clauses nor a future V1.2 is approved. No V1.2 file, experiment repository, experimental fixture, agent or experiment was created. The future experimental workspace remains separate and requires human scientific review and separate authorization.
