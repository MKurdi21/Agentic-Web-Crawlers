## Objective and evidence base

**Working question:** Does task-relevant framing of recurring website prerequisites increase bounded, independently verified non-progress and sustained retention relative to neutral framing on otherwise matched, solvable browser workflows? Can progress-aware escape/recovery reduce this burden without sacrificing legitimate task utility?

The objective is to investigate crawler-like adversarial traps against goal-directed AI web agents. The leading mechanism is a task-relevant recurring prerequisite that retains an agent without legitimate progress despite a reachable goal and usable escape. This is a working question and untested mechanism, not a publication title or demonstrated novel attack.

## Findings, competitors and provisional contribution

Classical traps establish useful analogies involving branching, changing identifiers, repeated content and delays. AI agents additionally interpret user goals and page semantics. Existing empirical studies demonstrate navigation diversion, task persuasion, iterative tool/resource manipulation and engagement with decoys; they do not all measure the same victim, task or outcome. Task relevance is not authorization, displayed progress is not verified progress, entry is not retention, and leaving/stopping is not task recovery.

The [expanded competitor comparison](../research/synthesis/TRAP_EXPERIMENT_LITERATURE_EVIDENCE_BRIDGE.md#competing-trap-mechanisms) distinguishes LoopTrap termination poisoning/recursive verification/circular prerequisites, Compliance Trap memory propagation, WebRollback recovery, ABxLab/Ads controlled behavioral interventions, hierarchical benign failures, and Silent Failures harness defects. When Agents Do Not Stop, Beyond Max Tokens and RecurGuard constrain resource/termination novelty. Source versions, physical locators and endpoint limitations remain explicit. The closest competition also includes POTEMKIN's citation-graph navigation traps, It's a TRAP!'s browser persuasion and chained illustration, Honeyval's AI hacking-agent engagement, LoginTrap's login prerequisites, AGENTBAIT's contextual verification pretexts, Telephone Loop's staged circular delegation, and Beyond Max Tokens / Overthinking Loops resource chains. Cloudflare AI Labyrinth is deployed crawler-decoy precedent. WebStep supplies semantic-state evaluation precedent. Verified publication identities, methods scope and supplemental/local distinctions are in the [roadmap](../research/synthesis/AI_WEB_AGENT_TRAP_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md#external-reference-verification--2026-10-16); the [evidence map](../research/synthesis/THEMATIC_EVIDENCE_MAP.md#focused-trap-comparison) compares access, duration and endpoints.

**Provisional contribution:** a controlled examination of task relevance × recurrence, independently measured non-progress and post-entry recovery, with an escape-versus-utility evaluation. None of these components is asserted individually new. Broad first-agent-trap, first-semantic-trap or first-deceptive-prerequisite claims are unsupported. Further competitor methods/artifact review may eliminate the proposed gap.

## Proposed threat model and hypotheses

One hostile site controls page text, links/forms and bounded session transitions. It knows public task/site context and ordinary object identifiers, not the private prompt, hidden reasoning, weights or memory. It cannot change trusted instructions, browser chrome/tools, the independent evaluator or resources outside the local sandbox. The primary agent uses DOM/accessibility observations; persistent cross-session memory is disabled initially. Every condition keeps the legitimate goal and escape reachable. Stronger internal-state, tool-server, visual or task-knowledge privileges are separate extensions.

- **H1 — primary candidate:** Task-relevant recurring prerequisites increase bounded non-progress interaction compared with neutral framing on equivalent recurring structures.
- **H2 — supporting interaction:** Task relevance interacts with recurrence, producing an effect beyond topology alone.
- **H3 — exploratory later intervention:** Appropriate progress-aware escape strategies can reduce non-progress work without substantially damaging legitimate iterative task performance.

The proposed feasibility pilot is exploratory, not a confirmatory result or a frozen protocol. The [gaps document](../research/synthesis/RESEARCH_GAPS_AND_HYPOTHESES.md) defines contrasts, falsifiers and secondary directions. The retained [standalone comparison design](../research/synthesis/CONTROLLED_COMPARISON_STUDY_DESIGN.md) is a separate secondary proposal with its original methodology intact.

## Evaluation and research sequence

Measure bounded decoy-directed decisions with no new verified legitimate subgoal across all assigned episodes, then exposure, first entry, entrant retention, escape, safe stop and independently verified completion after entry. Record requests, model calls, visible tokens, wall time and waiting separately. Capped exit times, operational missingness and crashes retain their distinct interpretations. Natural entrant-only comparisons are descriptive. Clean finite iterative tasks test whether a defense simply suppresses useful work.

The [roadmap](../research/synthesis/AI_WEB_AGENT_TRAP_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md) proposes six templates, four semantic × recurrence conditions plus a clean condition, two single-agent configurations and three repeats: **180 proposed episodes**, with **30 browser decisions, 60 model calls and five minutes maximum per episode**, plus explicit token/cost allowances to be fixed before execution. These are feasibility planning limits, not power evidence, implementation or dispatch authorization. Reactive H1 is primary; architecture comparisons are exploratory. A generic cap, cycle-aware crawler and rule-based navigator answer different comparator questions and must not share an unexplained susceptibility leaderboard.

Sequence: human contribution/threat-model review; separately authorized fixture feasibility; bounded pilot; then an independently specified confirmatory study if warranted. Progress-aware escape is the first later intervention. Memory, multimodal surfaces, adaptation and the standalone comparison remain supporting candidates. Null, topology-only and simple-effective-cap outcomes must remain reportable.

## Scientific qualifications and open decisions

Closest competitors may already contain the exact semantic/topological or recovery comparison in supplements. Public task context may be insufficient for the proposed manipulation. Semantic equivalence, genuine goal/exit reachability, oracle validity, clean-task utility and enough independent task families remain unproven. Synthetic-site results would not establish live-web prevalence. Model/service versions, observation visibility, caps and denominators require explicit control.

Retain unresolved source conflicts, including POTEMKIN thresholds, TRAP location aggregation, AgentLAB aggregates, Task Shield utility, SafeArena normalization and other issues indexed in [findings](../research/synthesis/INITIAL_RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md). AgentVigil/AgentFuzzer and the Mind the Web versions are not independent replications. Click, proposal, attempt, execution, judge approval and verified effects are not interchangeable ASRs. WebStep project-page and v2 abstract examples differ; do not silently pool them.
