# Comprehensive scientific literature synthesis

## 1. Research question and principal assessment

**Working research question:** Does task-relevant framing of recurring website prerequisites increase bounded, independently verified non-progress and sustained retention relative to neutral framing on otherwise matched, solvable browser workflows? Can progress-aware escape/recovery reduce this burden without sacrificing legitimate task utility?

The leading proposal examines task-dependent recurring prerequisites that sustain unproductive navigation despite a reachable legitimate goal and available escape. The question is provisional, not a fixed publication title. Existing studies already demonstrate navigation traps, task-based persuasion and iterative resource attacks. The proposed contribution is controlled explanation of task relevance, recurrence, non-progress and recovery, not a claimed first trap or a demonstrated new attack.

The earlier standalone-LLM/web-agent question is retained as supporting research. This corpus supports additional exposure routes and executable consequences, but does not establish a universal matched susceptibility increase. Section 9 and the [secondary study design](CONTROLLED_COMPARISON_STUDY_DESIGN.md) retain that analysis.

An agent repeatedly selects observations, incorporates some into its decision context, and converts outputs into operations with delegated authority. An attacker may influence one link without controlling the others. Retrieval placement is not instruction takeover; takeover is not necessarily an attempted forbidden call; an attempted call is not necessarily an executed harmful effect. Tools and memory can extend compromise, while isolation and authorization can constrain effects without curing the model. The unit of analysis is a configured model–interface–environment–permission system rather than an undifferentiated “agent.” [ReAct](../records/react_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims react_c01 through react_c15; physical PDF pp.2–23); [Indirect Prompt Injection in Real-World Applications](../records/indirect_prompt_injection_real_world_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C20; physical PDF pp.2–12); [AgentDojo](../records/agentdojo_a_dynamic_environment_to_evaluate_prompt_injection_attacks_and_defenses_for_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01/C07/C16; physical PDF pp.1–7); [CaMeL](../records/defeating_prompt_injections_by_design_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C02/C03/C05/C09; physical PDF pp.3–9,31–33).

This research-planning review is accompanied by the [evidence map](THEMATIC_EVIDENCE_MAP.md), [gap analysis](RESEARCH_GAPS_AND_HYPOTHESES.md) and [controlled-study proposal](CONTROLLED_COMPARISON_STUDY_DESIGN.md). It is not a publication manuscript or global novelty claim.

## 2. Corpus, method and evidentiary limits

The evidence base comprises 123 completed canonical records and comprehensive summaries: the preserved original 112 plus 11 admitted additions; five exact archived copies are separate. The extension used the existing Research Mode pipeline, full available PDF text/appendices and selected rendered pages, followed by source/claim review and individual acceptance receipts. Visual coverage limitations remain explicit. Original 112 outputs were not regenerated. The consolidated references provided navigation, while underlying records and selected typed claims supplied the analytical basis. Selected numbers and bibliographic points were checked against retained source extraction, and AgentDojo's defense table was checked directly in its canonical PDF. This is not a new page-by-page verification of every source, experiment reproduction or independent human validation of every extracted claim. Those source-check descriptions belong to the original synthesis; this synchronization revises its analysis and verifies identity/preservation without claiming that those checks were repeated.

This is a bounded collection, not a newly executed systematic search. Inclusion dates, benchmark versions, artifact availability and prior extraction coverage vary. Absence here does not establish global absence; paper frequency does not estimate real-world incidence. Survey corpora overlap primary studies. A citation, taxonomy cell and empirical trial are different observational units.

Evidence was assessed along distinct dimensions: attacker access; forced versus encountered exposure; intervention/comparator; measured stage; environment fidelity; uncertainty and evaluator validation; utility; and source completeness. A deterministic state check strengthens an endpoint without establishing representative deployment risk. A live demonstration improves ecological relevance without supplying a population denominator. Descriptive evidence labels in the map are not a numerical quality-ranking framework.

Two version families require explicit treatment. The canonical AgentFuzzer filename maps to an AGENTVIGIL v4 PDF related to published AgentVigil. The 13-page Mind the Web manuscript and 17-page publication are versions of one study. Version-specific cohorts, adaptive tests and judge evidence remain separate; shared results are not independent replication. Canonical records do not automatically represent independent teams or datasets. [AgentFuzzer](../records/agentfuzzer_generic_black_box_fuzzing_for_indirect_prompt_injection_against_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01_indirect_external_content through C15_derived_agentdojo_gain; physical PDF pp.1–14); [AgentVigil](../records/agentvigil_generic_black_box_red_teaming_for_indirect_prompt_injection_against_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims av_c01 through av_c15; physical PDF pp.2–14); [Mind the Web manuscript](../records/mind_the_web_the_security_of_web_use_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_16; physical PDF pp.1–13); [Mind the Web published](../records/mind_the_web_the_security_of_web_use_agents_5a834c4e_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C18; physical PDF pp.1–16).

Cross-paper explanations, evidence-strength judgments and causal assessments are analyst synthesis; they are not attributed as a shared author consensus. Study-specific methods and numbers are author-reported unless explicitly labeled analyst-derived.

Citations link unchanged source records containing typed claim IDs and physical PDF page locators. Broad claim ranges identify a record's evidence index; precise ranges accompany consequential numerical points. Author-reported observations are distinguished from this review's interpretations and hypotheses. Referenced external artifacts were not presumed inspected.

## Leading synthesis: from crawler tarpits to task-dependent retention

### Classical mechanisms and the semantic extension

Classical traps exploit branching, proliferating identifiers, repeated content, redirection or delayed responses. URL novelty is not useful progress, and a visited-URL set cannot detect every changing-content trap. The local [crawler-trap detection study](../records/detection_of_crawler_traps_formalization_and_implementation_defeating_protection_on_internet_and_on_the_tor_network_comprehensive_summary.json), C01–C14, physical pp.1–12, compares distributions over 13 families of 500 pages. It does not establish measured operational prevention by its proposed dynamic detector. [IRLbot](../records/irlbot_scaling_to_6_billion_pagesand_beyond_comprehensive_summary.json), irlbot_c01–irlbot_c14, physical pp.3–32, supplies scheduling/branching context. Cloudflare's deployed decoy account in the [roadmap](AI_WEB_AGENT_TRAP_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md) supplies implementation precedent, not evidence that visitors are goal-interpreting LLM agents.

AI web agents repeatedly interpret a trusted goal and semantic observations, choose actions, and incorporate feedback. A page can present another action as necessary to the goal: verify an item, repair a check, repeat validation, then receive a renewed dependency. The candidate mechanism is retention through apparently useful work. This is an analytical mechanism and proposed treatment, not an effect measured by this repository. A long legitimate workflow, slow server or incapable agent can produce similar traces. Identical page counts or repeated URLs alone cannot diagnose semantic trapping.

### What existing studies already establish

[POTEMKIN](../records/how_adversarial_environments_mislead_agentic_ai_comprehensive_summary.json), C01–C16, physical pp.2–17, already implements citation-graph navigation traps with compromised tool outputs, varied cycle length/plausibility, entry and wasted retrieval effort. Loops, dead ends and authority cascades are distinct. Its engagement-conditioned analyses and threshold inconsistencies constrain comparison. [It's a TRAP!](../records/its_a_trap_task_redirecting_agent_persuasion_benchmark_for_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), trap_claim_01–trap_claim_16, physical pp.3–19, studies task persuasion through editable browser content. Its main endpoint is initial clicked redirection; Figure 15 also illustrates chained continuation. It is inaccurate to say all prior work is single-step or purely syntactic injection.

[Beyond Max Tokens](../records/beyond_max_tokens_stealthy_resource_amplification_via_tool_calling_chains_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), C01–C15, physical pp.3–14, already uses progression, validation and termination chains through tool feedback. [Overthinking Loops](../records/overthinking_loops_in_agents_a_structural_risk_via_mcp_tools_comprehensive_summary.json), C01–C14, physical pp.1–12, induces repetition through malicious tool registries and returns. Their privileges and emulators differ from page-only browser control. [Breaking Agents](../records/breaking_agents_compromising_autonomous_llm_agents_through_malfunction_amplification_comprehensive_summary.json), C01–C14, physical pp.1–13, measures malfunction/self-reflected task failure; it does not supply an interchangeable browser-retention rate.

[Honeyval](../records/honeyval_a_comprehensive_evaluation_framework_for_llm_powered_http_honeypots_comprehensive_summary.json) measures AI hacking-agent engagement with HTTP decoys; [LoginTrap](../records/logintrap_uncovering_task_agnostic_phishing_style_indirect_prompt_injection_attacks_against_llm_based_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) uses context-derived authentication prerequisites. Both are now canonical source-bound additions. AGENTBAIT and Telephone Loop remain supplemental competition, using contextual verification/finalization pretexts and staged circular delegation. The [focused evidence comparison](THEMATIC_EVIDENCE_MAP.md#focused-trap-comparison) separates access and endpoints. Supplemental status is not canonical inclusion, and a canonical record is not independent replication.

Continuation and dependency mechanisms are already close prior art. [LoopTrap](../records/looptrap_termination_poisoning_attacks_on_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–4, 11–13, 16, 19–21 explicitly includes recursive verification and circular prerequisites, reporting finite step/token amplification in simulated tool returns. It also proposes independent progress verification as future work. The proposed contribution therefore requires a causal semantic-versus-topological comparison in solvable browser tasks, conditional retention, reachable exits and independently checked recovery; neither a new verification pretext nor a generic progress oracle establishes novelty. [Compliance Trap](../records/the_compliance_trap_diagnosing_how_ai_agents_consume_conflicting_memory_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9, 14, 22–25 supplies an identifiability × groundability factorial and distinguishes memory compliance, divergence, trajectory realignment and final success. Its system/footer interventions occupy a different privilege tier from actual page delivery.

### Progress deception, retention and recovery

The proposed distinction is between page-claimed advancement and independent legitimate task progress. A changing verification number can look productive while the legitimate goal state remains unchanged. An analyst must distinguish exposure, first entry, decoy-directed non-progress decisions, departure, safe stop and verified completion after entry. Recovery requires the legitimate task to finish; refusal or leaving the decoy is not by itself recovery. A legitimate goal and escape must remain reachable under every treatment. All are future measurement requirements.

Resource effects follow behavior but do not define the mechanism: calls, browser requests, visible tokens, elapsed time and waiting must be reported separately. A cap bounds observed work and censors natural exit time; it does not prove an infinite loop. Operational interruptions, crashes and valid safe stops remain separate terminal states. Entry-conditioned groups are selected by the treatment, so their retention differences are descriptive unless a separate forced-entry design randomizes continuation after a common starting state.

Independent semantic-state evaluation has relevant precedent. [WebStep](../records/where_did_it_go_wrong_process_level_evaluation_of_web_agents_with_semantic_state_tracking_comprehensive_summary.json) records task-state transitions separately from agent-facing GUI interaction. The supplied workshop version is now canonical; its numerical disagreements with public versions and SafePass terminal criteria are retained. This is benign evaluation work, not a demonstrated trap defense. Verify subgoals outside attacker-controlled claims while auditing the oracle itself.

### Defenses and open questions

Termination caps limit burden; URL/cycle detection handles some structures; progress-aware retry and escape rules may identify semantic recurrence. Each can also truncate valid iteration. Task Shield and planning guards motivate goal-contribution checks, while external enforcement can constrain side effects without detecting time wasted on allowed actions (section 7). Neither those defenses nor a combined stack is validated for this proposed trap. The proper comparison includes clean finite iterative tasks, a generic action cap and cycle-aware controls, with all assigned cases retained.

The [roadmap](AI_WEB_AGENT_TRAP_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md) proposes a task-relevance × recurrence pilot, including a matched clean condition, a reactive agent and an exploratory planner/executor configuration. H1 tests the semantic contrast on recurring structures; H2 tests interaction beyond topology; H3 later tests escape versus legitimate utility. Six templates × five conditions × two configurations × three repeats gives 180 proposed episodes, not independent task families or a powered confirmatory study. Page length, delay, task difficulty, attacker knowledge and available exits are competing explanations. A null or topology-only result must remain publishable evidence rather than trigger post hoc methodology changes.

The open scientific questions are whether semantics changes sustained work beyond entry, whether that effect survives matched graph/content controls, whether public task context suffices, and whether a simple progress-aware bound preserves ordinary task utility. Novelty requires checking competitors' methods and trajectories, including unavailable supplements. The 123-record corpus and a targeted external search cannot establish worldwide absence.

## 3. Architecture, observation and delegated authority

ReAct establishes an observation–reasoning–action loop; LASER makes action availability state-dependent; SeeAct separates intended action from element grounding. These differences matter because a payload can target planning, selection or arguments separately. An action vocabulary constrains expression but does not prove that an expressible action serves the user. BrowserGym further includes configurations mapping generated output into executable browser code. These are candidate boundaries inferred from capability designs, not adversarial findings in those architectural papers. [ReAct](../records/react_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims react_c01 through react_c15; physical PDF pp.2–23); [LASER](../records/laser_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims laser_claim_01 through laser_claim_16; physical PDF pp.2–12); [SeeAct](../records/seeact_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims seeact_c01 through seeact_c16; physical PDF pp.3–25); [BrowserGym](../records/browsergym_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims BG-C01 through BG-C16; physical PDF pp.1–33).

Interfaces differ materially. AutoWebGLM simplifies HTML and uses optical character recognition; WebVoyager consumes screenshots, element descriptions and downloaded-PDF text; VisualWebArena combines images, accessibility information and Set-of-Mark labels. Mind2Web and WebLINX score decisions against static states/histories rather than arbitrary alternative trajectories. A hidden-attribute payload is consequently not available or equivalent in every screenshot system. Wrappers, summarizers and captioners can transform content before it reaches the actor. [AutoWebGLM](../records/autowebglm_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims autowebglm_c01 through autowebglm_c15; physical PDF pp.1–12); [WebVoyager](../records/webvoyager_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims WV-C01 through WV-C15; physical PDF pp.2–27); [VisualWebArena](../records/visualwebarena_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims vwa_c01 through vwa_c15; physical PDF pp.1–25); [Mind2Web](../records/mind2web_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims mind2web_c01 through mind2web_c15; physical PDF pp.5–22); [WebLINX](../records/weblinx_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims weblinx_c01 through weblinx_c14; physical PDF pp.1–50); [Dissecting Adversarial Robustness](../records/dissecting_adversarial_robustness_of_multimodal_lm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–22).

Authority must be reconstructed from the actual action set. WebGPT searches, follows existing links and collects quotations without arbitrary form submission or direct editing. ReAct excludes real purchases and Wikipedia edits; LASER stops Amazon trials at the buying decision; SeeAct restricts sensitive final actions and uses human review. WebArena includes authenticated state changes. These conditions prevent equating all web access with unrestricted write permission. [WebGPT](../records/webgpt_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims webgpt_c01 through webgpt_c15; physical PDF pp.1–28); [ReAct](../records/react_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims react_c01 through react_c15; physical PDF pp.2–23); [LASER](../records/laser_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims laser_claim_01 through laser_claim_16; physical PDF pp.2–12); [SeeAct](../records/seeact_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims seeact_c01 through seeact_c16; physical PDF pp.3–25); [WebArena](../records/webarena_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims WA_C01 through WA_C15; physical PDF pp.2–22).

Training/exploration adds other dependencies. Go-Browse selects model-judged successful trajectories; WebDancer/WebSailor filter reasoning/action data; AutoScraper/WebScraper generate extraction paths or executable code. Toolformer tests learned API use and enabled/disabled capability ablations; Gorilla distinguishes API correctness from program execution. These motivate security investigation of evidence selection, training and code boundaries, but do not establish that those boundaries were exploited. Success-conditioned training and judging errors are not themselves compromises. [Go-Browse](../records/go_browse_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims GB-C01 through GB-C16; physical PDF pp.1–20); [WebDancer](../records/webdancer_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims WD_C01 through WD_C14; physical PDF pp.1–24); [WebSailor](../records/websailor_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims websailor_c01 through websailor_c15; physical PDF pp.1–23); [AutoScraper](../records/autoscraper_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims autoscraper_c01 through autoscraper_c15; physical PDF pp.5–19); [WebScraper](../records/webscraper_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims webscraper_c01 through webscraper_c16; physical PDF pp.2–6); [Toolformer](../records/toolformer_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims toolformer_claim_01 through toolformer_claim_16; physical PDF pp.1–17); [Gorilla](../records/gorilla_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims gorilla_c01 through gorilla_c13; physical PDF pp.4–21).

Shopping, enterprise and multihop benchmarks also define different kinds of legitimate competence. WebShop evaluates simulated product selection rather than completed real purchases; WorkArena tests enterprise workflows with model-specific configurations; MMINA requires ordered multihop completion. Their failures can identify planning or grounding bottlenecks, but neither those failures nor their differing task scores isolate malicious-input susceptibility. [WebShop](../records/webshop_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims webshop_c01 through webshop_c12; physical PDF pp.1–10); [WorkArena](../records/workarena_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims workarena_c01 through workarena_c17; physical PDF pp.1–21); [MMINA](../records/mmina_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims mmina_c01 through mmina_c14; physical PDF pp.2–13).

Observation delivery is an empirical variable. [Machine-Readable Ads](../records/machine_readable_ads_accessibility_and_trust_patterns_for_ai_web_agents_interacting_with_online_advertisements_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–8 reports semantic button/offscreen-text effects on one cloned site, while pixel-only text often receives no clicks; Operator and BrowserUse differ in instrumentation and confirmations. Accessibility or commercially induced choice is not automatically malicious injection. [ABxLab](../records/a_framework_for_studying_ai_agent_behavior_evidence_from_consumer_choice_experiments_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–9, 16–25 randomizes consumer-choice nudges with matched-rating/price regimes, but measures simulated choice rather than checkout, welfare or compromise. Human comparisons use a different interface and Original products only. [A Whole New World](../records/a_whole_new_world_creating_a_parallel_poisoned_web_only_ai_agents_can_see_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9 reports an internal Cursor authentication demonstration with shell/environment-secret access; its unspecified trial denominator cannot establish a general attack rate or measured cloaking accuracy.

## 4. Exposure and environmental instruction injection

Indirect prompt injection exploits a mismatch between a source's authority and the authority attributed to its contents. The foundational application study inserts commands into retrieved data without participating in the user's conversation. Its selected propagation, search and memory examples demonstrate routes, but prepared tools, local HTML and synthetic retrieval do not supply a general exploitation rate. Formalizing Prompt Injection controls instruction/data tasks, yet its mixed task-score average is not a deployed-agent compromise probability. Together they establish an authority problem without estimating a universal browser-integration effect. [Indirect Prompt Injection in Real-World Applications](../records/indirect_prompt_injection_real_world_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C20; physical PDF pp.2–12); [Formalizing Prompt Injection](../records/formalizing_prompt_injection_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.2–14).

Exposure itself requires measurement. SearchGeo, FORGE and Retrieval Barrier investigate placement, source count or retriever similarity under controlled encounters. ToolHijacker separates retrieval from malicious selection. Retrieval Barrier's email condition can have recall 1.0 and a particular objective's ASR 0.02, illustrating why context entry and influence need separate measures. Canary tokens observe source-use/attribution evidence; appearances after access restrictions do not uniquely determine cache ownership, a fresh crawl or training memorization. [SearchGeo](../records/searchgeo_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims searchgeo_c01 through searchgeo_c15; physical PDF pp.1–22); [FORGE](../records/forge_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims forge_c01 through forge_c16; physical PDF pp.1–26); [Retrieval Barrier](../records/retrieval_barrier_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C18; physical PDF pp.1–20); [ToolHijacker](../records/toolhijacker_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims TH-C01 through TH-C15; physical PDF pp.1–17); [Canary Tokens](../records/canary_tokens_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims canary_claim_01 through canary_claim_15; physical PDF pp.4–16).

Task relevance is not authorization. AdvAgent preserves operation/element while redirecting arguments; WASP uses contributor content without layout control; Mind the Web validates apparently helpful candidates through agent interaction before training an attack generator. AGENTVIGIL searches adaptively using assisted mutations. Their access and budgets differ, so they are not replications of one attack. Their common implication is narrower: useful-looking content can compete with user intent even when unrelated instructions would be easier to reject. [AdvAgent](../records/advagent_controllable_blackbox_red_teaming_on_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–13); [WASP](../records/wasp_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims wasp_c01 through wasp_c16; physical PDF pp.3–22); [Mind the Web published](../records/mind_the_web_the_security_of_web_use_agents_5a834c4e_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C18; physical PDF pp.1–16); [AgentFuzzer](../records/agentfuzzer_generic_black_box_fuzzing_for_indirect_prompt_injection_against_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01_indirect_external_content through C15_derived_agentdojo_gain; physical PDF pp.1–14); [AgentVigil](../records/agentvigil_generic_black_box_red_teaming_for_indirect_prompt_injection_against_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims av_c01 through av_c15; physical PDF pp.2–14).

Deployed examples complement benchmark evidence. Commercial-agent experiments include controlled form submission and a test executable; When AI Meets the Web traces integration paths promoting external text into privileged context. WAAA examines cross-origin consequences mediated by permitted actions, not a native browser-isolation exploit. Search endorsement, unsafe recommendation and fabricated answers in FORGE, Unsafe LLM Search and POTEMKIN are information failures, not independently verified installation, external receipt or financial loss. Ecological relevance does not erase endpoint distinctions. [Commercial LLM Agents](../records/commercial_llm_agents_are_already_vulnerable_to_simple_yet_dangerous_attacks_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C14; physical PDF pp.1–10); [When AI Meets the Web](../records/when_ai_meets_web_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_14; physical PDF pp.3–20); [WAAA](../records/waaa_web_adversaries_against_agentic_browsers_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims waaa_c01 through waaa_c14; physical PDF pp.4–14); [FORGE](../records/forge_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims forge_c01 through forge_c16; physical PDF pp.1–26); [Unsafe LLM Search](../records/unsafe_llm_search_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims unsafe_search_c01 through unsafe_search_c16; physical PDF pp.1–21); [POTEMKIN](../records/how_adversarial_environments_mislead_agentic_ai_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C16; physical PDF pp.2–17).

TRAP makes persuasion and initial redirection explicit: its 3780 attacked runs contain 948 successful redirects and 639 separate step-cap terminations. A click diversion is not completed credential theft, and a termination is not automatically successful resistance. This is consistent with the need to record attacker objective, ordinary failure and verified side effect separately rather than classifying every interrupted task as compromise or safety. [TRAP](../records/its_a_trap_task_redirecting_agent_persuasion_benchmark_for_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims trap_claim_01 through trap_claim_16; physical PDF pp.3–19).

**Exposure assessment:** architectural and experimental evidence supports additional conditional entry routes. Their natural encounter frequency and population distribution remain less well isolated. Forced encounters estimate outcomes under exposure, not deployment-wide risk.

## 5. Visual, tool and persistent-state mechanisms

### 5.1 Perception as a separate intervention point

Visual and accessibility channels can disagree. Pop-ups conditions on selected tasks and default knowledge of user query, framework and target location; suppressed clicks do not execute the malicious destination. EIA evaluates private-value entry on177 curated offline steps: its 70% specific-information peak and 16% full-request peak use different placement, opacity and attack conditions. AdvAgent's97.5%/99.8% domain-average step results report domain variation rather than repeated-run uncertainty. None estimates completed harmful transactions across all real users. [Pop-ups](../records/attacking_vision_language_computer_agents_via_pop_ups_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims claim_01 through claim_15; physical PDF pp.2–15); [EIA](../records/eia_environmental_injection_attack_on_generalist_web_agents_for_privacy_leakage_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims eia_claim_01 through eia_claim_14; physical PDF pp.4–32); [AdvAgent](../records/advagent_controllable_blackbox_red_teaming_on_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json) (claims C01 through C15; physical PDF pp.1–13).

Dissecting Adversarial Robustness separates captioner from actor success: reported GPT-4o caption success 92% accompanies agent ASR 31% in a selected forced-exposure setting. WebInject requires page-source modification and white-box model access and scores exact next-action output. DUDE uses static screenshot click boxes; WebCloak reverses the victim perspective by disrupting a scraper. Pixel, caption, HTML and accessibility control are different experimental factors, not one multimodal severity scale. ## 10. Conflicts and research priorities

Some apparent disagreement follows from different treatments: a selector is not an answer decoder; a pixel perturbation is not a contributor edit; a native skill stack is not a no-skill baseline; benign difficulty is not injection refusal. Other discrepancies remain unresolved: AgentLAB aggregates, Task Shield utility tables, LASM coding/defense counts, DUDE budgets/splits, AWE cost arithmetic, Gorilla counts/metrics, EvoCrawl coverage/versions and POTEMKIN thresholds. The evidence map retains the ambiguity rather than choosing a convenient value.

Surveys organize questions but do not replace primary evidence.
