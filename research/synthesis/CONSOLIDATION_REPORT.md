## Scientific qualifications retained

Exposure, model susceptibility and executable consequences remain separate. Detector accuracy, proposed/attempted action, static click, simulated tool invocation, verified side effect and benign failure retain their different meanings. Incompatible ASRs are not ranked or pooled. Enforcement assumptions, security/utility losses and untested adaptive/combined-stack claims remain explicit.

AgentVigil/AgentFuzzer and both Mind the Web versions stay separately traceable without being independent replications. Five archived exact copies do not increase the corpus count. Survey statistics are secondary evidence. Gorilla's no-retrieval result is clarified by its training condition rather than conflated with an oracle-trained model missing documentation.

Unresolved evidence includes Task Shield utility tables; AgentLAB aggregates; SafeArena normalization; LASM assignment/defense counts and theorem premises; DUDE cohort/budget conflicts; AWE cost arithmetic; Gorilla API/metric inconsistencies; EvoCrawl coverage/version conflicts; POTEMKIN thresholds; and Mind the Web judge descriptions. Their intended corrections were not guessed. Other limitations remain in the thematic discussion and unchanged source records.

The reviewed evidence still does not establish a universal otherwise matched standalone-LLM versus web-agent vulnerability rate. Mechanism-based interpretations and future hypotheses remain qualified; no global novelty or consensus claim was introduced.
