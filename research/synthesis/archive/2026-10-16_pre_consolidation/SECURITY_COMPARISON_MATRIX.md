# Preliminary security comparison matrix

Coverage: 112 validated canonical Research Mode records: 93 prior completions and19
final19-continuation additions. All112 canonical output pairs are complete;0 pending,0 excluded,0 failed.
This is a preliminary comparison, not a final literature review or independent
human scientific validation. References use physical PDF pages and record claim IDs.

**Evidence labels:** demonstrated = evaluated behavior under a stated protocol;
author risk = a proposed or acknowledged concern; inference = analyst interpretation.
A boundary inferred from architecture is not itself a demonstrated exploit.
“Not evaluated” refers to the supplied paper, not a guarantee of safety.
Capability scores are omitted from security outcomes. Attack percentages from
different protocols must not be ranked or pooled.

defense effectiveness are not experimentally evaluated. The boundaries below are
analyst security interpretations of described architectures. Human assistance,
restricted actions and modeling filters are not automatically validated defenses.
