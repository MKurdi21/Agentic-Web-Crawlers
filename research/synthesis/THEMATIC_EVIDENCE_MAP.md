# Thematic evidence map

## 1. Purpose and comparison rules

This map links the 123 canonical records to the analytical review. Tables place related studies alongside one another, but rows are **not a leaderboard**. Author-reported findings retain their access, denominator, baseline and endpoint. Citation ranges refer to typed claims and physical PDF pages in unchanged records. The final register provides exactly one inventory entry per canonical record.

Evidence descriptions distinguish: intermediate manipulation; action proposal/attempt; source-verified executed effects in a controlled environment; hypothetical risk; benign capability/failure; detector output; empirical defense intervention; and proposed defense. These are endpoint types, not an ordinal quality score. “Executed” denotes the paper's evidence, not an independently reproduced experiment. A classifier trial cannot establish protected task completion; permission appearance is not a harmful outcome.

Study families: AgentFuzzer/AGENTVIGIL and published AgentVigil are related versions; both Mind the Web records represent one study. Shared results do not supply independent replication. Surveys overlap primary sources and are not counted as additional experiments. Five exact archived copies are outside the 123 canonical identities.

## Focused trap comparison

**Current working question:** Does task-relevant framing of recurring website prerequisites increase bounded, independently verified non-progress and sustained retention relative to neutral framing on otherwise matched, solvable browser workflows? Can progress-aware escape/recovery reduce this burden without sacrificing legitimate task utility? The [roadmap](AI_WEB_AGENT_TRAP_RESEARCH_ROADMAP.md) is the leading proposal. This focused comparison selects related mechanisms; it does not make every canonical paper a trap study or replace the complete register below. All results are author-reported, not reproduced here. Unreported duration, progress and resource quantities remain unknown.

## 12. Reading the map as a whole

For the current trap question, compare semantics with topology and observe legitimate progress, termination and recovery independently; see H1–H3 in the [gaps](RESEARCH_GAPS_AND_HYPOTHESES.md). Existing trap, persuasion and resource studies limit broad novelty. The strongest cross-study convergence is mechanistic: untrusted information can influence decisions, and authority determines which effects can follow. The strongest qualification is methodological: unlike exposure, threat access, endpoints, budgets and competence make simple ASR ordering invalid. Proposed controls should be compared on their own security/utility frontiers and stated assumptions. Neither the record count nor repeated citations establish independent replication.
