## Scientific concerns

Attack rates are kept under their original denominators, budgets and protocols. Tool selection, attempted action, broad semantic success, task failure and completed harmful execution are distinguished. Security and capability/utility outcomes are separated. Unresolved source conflicts are preserved rather than guessed.

Notable review flags include SafeArena normalized-score arithmetic, WebWalker conditional-action aggregation, ST-WebAgentBench pass@3/risk definitions, Overthinking Loops ratio aggregation, and table/prose discrepancies in several papers. Hidden in Memory’s broad judge and conditional saved-memory tests do not prove every reported success is completed harm. Crawler-trap interval overlap is not measured classifier error. AgentFuzzer’s canonical mapping contains AGENTVIGILv4, a version-related identity requiring care against independent-study double counting. See the individual summaries and source review for page-specific details.

No addition establishes an otherwise matched standalone-LLM versus web-agent adversarial security comparison. Toolformer has a same-model tools-enabled/disabled capability ablation, which does not answer the central security question.
