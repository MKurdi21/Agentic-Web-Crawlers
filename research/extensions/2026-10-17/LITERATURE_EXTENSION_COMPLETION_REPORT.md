## Material discrepancies and interpretation limits

- **LoopTrap (physical PDF pp.3–4,11–13,16,19–21):**recursive verification/circular prerequisites and task-grounded continuation are prior art. Finite SAF/TAF amplification is not infinity or browser dwell. Strict/inclusive threshold, category/profiling/aggregate inconsistencies and changed-answer example are retained.
- **Compliance Trap (physical PDF pp.4–9,14,22–25):**system/footer memory injection differs from page-only attack. Selected77-task WebArena pool, conditional compliance versus divergence metrics, varying validN and Gemini scope conflict limit generalization. Appendix15-step/600-second caps are explicit. Realignment can coexist with 1.3% final success. Factorial identifiability/groundability is prior art; retry requires failure oracle/extra attempt; Table29 captions/rows disagree.
- **WebStep (physical PDF pp.3–8,17–18,35,48):**the placed workshop PDF differs numerically from the public abstract/project. Appendix SafePass applies to all terminal success rates and can accept an informed decision without final commitment; modality varies by agent. Recorded oracle traces are not guaranteed globally minimal.
- **Silent Failures (physical PDF pp.3–7):**delivery4/43→43/43 and identical-trace ASR21.7→1.2% changes demonstrate why harness audits matter. Tool-name UPR is weaker than task completion, predicates do not necessarily prove harm, source missing-payload wording/rounded differences conflict, and full suites/results are withheld. The claimed release is not independently reproduced.
- **ABxLab (physical PDF pp.3–9,16–25):**randomized choice nudges and matched-rating/price regimes are useful design precedents; composition differs across regimes and humans use Original products/another interface. Percentage-point choice shifts are not attack success, checkout or welfare loss.
- **LoginTrap (physical PDF pp.4–11):**login entry, synthetic sensitive-field input and any-success-over-nine PER are distinct. Five-step runs cannot establish sustained retention. Submission is not uniformly required; no actual credential theft is established. Detailed defense configurations/results are missing.
- **Machine-Readable Ads (physical PDF pp.4–9):**markup/observation accessibility and goal-contingent synthetic subscription behavior are not automatically attacks or actual financial loss. BrowserUse/Operator modalities and instrumentation differ. Scroll-summary and conditional subscription accounting conflicts remain unresolved.
- **Honeyval (physical PDF pp.4–8,12):**AI hacking agents pursue impossible exploit goals on resource-free HTTP decoys. Request count measures engagement, not useful intelligence, browser recovery or prompt-disclosure success. Cost estimates omit long-context pricing tiers; detection needs FPR context and fidelity is separate from engagement.
- **WebRollback (physical PDF pp.2–5,8–11):**actual rollback restores a recorded URL, not arbitrary session/server/database state. Benign judge-based success and fewer navigation steps do not prove adversarial escape or fewer model calls; live-site filtering and permissive judge instructions limit interpretation.
- **Hierarchical planning (physical PDF pp.5–7,15,19–22):**benign plan/execution/replanning failures are alternatives to attack-caused recurrence. Model-as-judge agreement is sampled author evidence (41/50 postconditions,43/50 final outcomes), not ground truth. NL alignment, PDDL baseline and Gemini labels conflict in the source.
- **A Whole New World (physical PDF pp.4–9):**conceptual delivery/cloaking model plus a reported small internal Cursor demonstration; trial denominators, classifier accuracy, controlled effectiveness and evaluated mitigation are absent. Three listed model configurations do not license n=3 or100% ASR; shell/environment-secret access is a stronger privilege tier.
