## Already established findings

- **Task-compatible prerequisites and termination poisoning:** [LoopTrap](../../records/looptrap_termination_poisoning_attacks_on_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–4,11–13,16,19–21 includes recursive decomposition, dependency chains and task-grounded continuation; [LoginTrap](../../records/logintrap_uncovering_task_agnostic_phishing_style_indirect_prompt_injection_attacks_against_llm_based_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9,11 already makes authentication appear necessary using public page context. [It’s a TRAP!](../../records/its_a_trap_task_redirecting_agent_persuasion_benchmark_for_web_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3,5–9,13,15 includes contextual tailoring,639 cap terminations and a chained-continuation illustration. A new pretext is insufficient novelty.
- **Topology and selective environmental content:** [POTEMKIN](../../records/how_adversarial_environments_mislead_agentic_ai_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–8,15–17 already studies citation-graph cycles/dead ends, framing and engagement-conditioned effects. [A Whole New World](../../records/a_whole_new_world_creating_a_parallel_poisoned_web_only_ai_agents_can_see_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9 combines selective delivery with a small reported secret-authentication demonstration; measured fingerprint accuracy, stealth and rate remain absent.
- **Memory uptake and recovery are separable:** [Compliance Trap](../../records/the_compliance_trap_diagnosing_how_ai_agents_consume_conflicting_memory_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9,14,22–25 distinguishes entry/propagation/realignment from final success and already includes a 2×2 identifiability×groundability experiment. Its system/footer intervention is a different access tier from actual webpage delivery.
- **Progress and recovery methods exist:** [WebStep](../../records/where_did_it_go_wrong_process_level_evaluation_of_web_agents_with_semantic_state_tracking_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–8,17–18,35 implements evaluator-side semantic-state/process diagnosis; its SafePass is not necessarily committed completion. [Hierarchical Planning](../../records/why_do_llm_based_web_agents_fail_a_hierarchical_planning_perspective_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–7,15,19–21 decomposes benign planning/execution/replanning and samples judge agreement. [WebRollback](../../records/webrollback_enhancing_web_agents_with_explicit_rollback_mechanisms_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.2–5,8–11 provides when/where URL rollback with partial/full task and state-switch metrics.
- **Engagement and resource amplification exist:** [Honeyval](../../records/honeyval_a_comprehensive_evaluation_framework_for_llm_powered_http_honeypots_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–8,12,16 measures hacking-agent HTTP engagement with impossible exploit goals and separate capability/fidelity controls. [Beyond Max Tokens](../../records/beyond_max_tokens_stealthy_resource_amplification_via_tool_calling_chains_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–9,13–14 measures tool-chain resource amplification under controlled emulation. [RecurGuard](../../records/recurguard_runtime_monitoring_for_reasoning_token_consumption_attacks_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.5,7,10–14 provides scoped token-monitor results and adaptive boundaries; [When Agents Do Not Stop](../../records/when_agents_do_not_stop_uncovering_infinite_agentic_loops_in_llm_agents_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3,7–10 identifies static feedback-bound failures.
- **Observation and framing interventions exist:** [Machine-Readable Ads](../../records/machine_readable_ads_accessibility_and_trust_patterns_for_ai_web_agents_interacting_with_online_advertisements_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.4–9 shows DOM/pixel/runtime differences and synthetic goal-contingent choices; [ABxLab](../../records/a_framework_for_studying_ai_agent_behavior_evidence_from_consumer_choice_experiments_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–9,16–25 supplies randomized nudges and attribute-matching designs. Choice shifts are not automatically harmful ASR.
- **Harness audits are established requirements:** [Silent Failures](../../records/silent_failures_in_agentic_security_evaluation_a_validated_harness_for_tool_call_mediation_under_indirect_prompt_injection_comprehensive_summary.json), physical PDF pp.3–7 shows delivery/scorer/capability confounding. Its UPR is required-tool coverage, not independently verified user-goal success; argument predicates need not prove downstream harm.
